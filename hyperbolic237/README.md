# The `*237` reflection group and the Klein quartic

This is a separate, **runnable hyperbolic experiment** alongside Conway's existing
Euclidean `.pattern` language. It constructs a Schwarz triangle, reflects it in
the Poincaré disk, computes an **exact finite quotient** of the infinite triangle
group, and derives the `{7,3}` regular map of the genus-three Klein quartic.

The generator emits an SVG of a Poincaré-disk tiling with **24 exact quotient
heptagon labels** (repeated across the universal cover).

## Run (Node.js 22+, no packages)

```sh
node hyperbolic237/render.mjs json
node hyperbolic237/render.mjs svg klein-237.svg
node hyperbolic237/render-domain.mjs svg klein-237-fundamental-polygon.svg
node hyperbolic237/render-domain.mjs json klein-237-pairings.json
node --test hyperbolic237/tests/*.test.mjs
```

The SVG colors come from exact right cosets in PGL(2,7). The image displays a
**patch of the universal cover**, not an embedding of the closed Riemann
surface. The outer white ring shows where the finite patch stops.

## Explicit genus-three fundamental polygon

The [88-side geometric fundamental polygon](FUNDAMENTAL-POLYGON.md) is the next
step beyond finite coset coloring. Its 336 exact chamber representatives have
460 genuinely shared internal edges and a single **88-side boundary cycle**;
44 pairs of boundary edges have explicit, orientation-preserving kernel words.
A faithful **exact Tits representation** over `Z[2 cos(pi/7)]` decides equality
in the *infinite* reflection group. The disk coordinates are numerical only.
Identifying all sides gives `V=164`, `E=504`, `F=336`, hence genus three.
The new renderer outputs an SVG and the full pairing table as JSON.

## The group structure

Let `r0`, `r1`, `r2` reflect the sides AB, AC, BC of the triangle with corner
angles A=π/2, B=π/3, C=π/7, respectively. The full reflection group has
presentation

```
r0² = r1² = r2² = 1
(r0 r1)² = 1
(r0 r2)³ = 1
(r1 r2)⁷ = 1
```

In PGL(2,F7), take U=[[1,1],[0,1]], V=[[0,-1],[1,0]], and
D=[[-1,0],[0,1]]. Mapping `r0 → V D`, `r1 → D`, `r2 → D U` satisfies these
relations **exactly**, with respective product orders 2, 3, and 7.
The generated finite projective group has 336 elements. Its determinant-square
subgroup is PSL(2,7) of order 168. The kernel within the orientation-preserving
triangle group is torsion-free, because each of the three torsion generators
retains its exact order in the quotient; by the classification of torsion in
triangle groups, it determines a closed hyperbolic surface.

In the quotient, each heptagon consists of fourteen Schwarz triangles. The
subgroup generated by `r1`, `r2` is a dihedral-14 stabilizer of the sevenfold
corner. Therefore 336/14 = **24 heptagons**. Separately, oriented darts are
PSL(2,7), and right multiplication by U (face turn), V (edge reversal), and
(VU)⁻¹ (vertex turn) yields exact cycles:

| Quotient data | Count | Orbit size |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Faces | 24 | 7 |
| Edges | 84 | 2 |
| Vertices | 56 | 3 |

Euler characteristic = 56 − 84 + 24 = −4, hence genus 3. Each Schwarz triangle
has hyperbolic area π/42, so the quotient area is 336π/42 = 8π, consistent
with Gauss–Bonnet.

## Important boundaries

- **Exact:** all F7 projective matrices, generator relations, subgroup sizes,
  orbits, and map counts. A numerical reflection matrix is never treated as a
  certificate for a Coxeter relation.
- **Approximate:** the Poincaré disk vertices, geodesic arcs and SVG rendering.
  The tiling engine suppresses approximate duplicate chambers only for drawing,
  and checks that their *exact* finite labels agree.
- **Not implemented:** a general hyperbolic `orbifold` command in `.pattern`,
  a normal-form algorithm for the infinite Coxeter group, a **minimal** compact 12- or 14-gon, or a faithful
  3D Klein-quartic embedding. The 24
  coset-colors visualize its regular map; they do not by themselves produce
  a geometric quotient mesh.
- **Integration:** `Conway/pattern-language` currently represents Euclidean
  affine actions as six-number matrices. Hyperbolic isometries require a
  different representation; this folder deliberately does not overload that
  affine matrix type.
